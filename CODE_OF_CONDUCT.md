# Community conduct

In Issues, pull requests, and other project spaces, discuss the problem and evidence respectfully. Critique ideas and code; avoid insults, harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, and publishing another person's private information. Do not post credentials or sensitive container logs.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. If conduct needs attention, report the offending content through GitHub's reporting tools; do not repost private information in a public Issue. Security vulnerabilities belong in [private vulnerability reports](SECURITY.md).
